**Race:** Half-Elf  
**Class:** Minstrel  
**Instrument:** [[Artifacts-&-Treasures#Alvion's Hurdy-gurdy|Hurdy-gurdy]]

## Description

A flamboyantly attired entertainer whose skill with the hurdy-gurdy proves as effective at distracting mobs as [[Ferguson-MacJayne|MacJayne]]'s blade is at dispatching them. His music can charm both friend and foe alike.

## Notable Traits

- Musical enchantment
- Crowd control
- Flamboyant style
